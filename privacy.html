WordGames Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

WordGames is a set of daily word puzzles. This policy explains what information the app handles. It applies to the WordGames iPhone app.

Your puzzles stay on your iPhone

Your puzzle progress, streaks, statistics, hints and settings are stored only on your device. WordGames has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer does not receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows occasional ads from Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, Google may also use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads; if you don't, it won't. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads, and you can change that choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

WordGames Plus turns ads off completely; with Plus the app doesn't load ads.

Purchases

WordGames Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; the app only receives confirmation of whether Plus was purchased.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone as a local notification. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

WordGames is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at https://skydiverredman.github.io/wordgames-site/privacy.html. For questions, email skydiverredman.app@gmail.com.
