Support

Need a hand, or found a bug? Email skydiverredman.app@gmail.com.

How do streaks work?

Finish all three daily puzzles to extend your streak. A Daily Guess you didn't solve still counts as finished. Practice, Free Play and past crosswords don't affect it.

I bought WordGames Plus but still see ads

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. You can also restore from the WordGames Plus screen.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I'm not getting my daily reminder

Turn the reminder on in the app's Settings, then check that notifications are allowed for WordGames in the iPhone Settings app. It only arrives on days you haven't finished the Daily Mix.

Where is my progress stored?

Your streaks, statistics and puzzle progress stay on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings in the app and look under Privacy. WordGames Plus turns ads off completely.
